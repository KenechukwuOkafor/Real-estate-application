# Notifications Domain Specification

## Purpose

The Notifications domain governs all user-facing communications generated by the Ruvo platform.

Notifications inform users of important platform events, required actions, and status updates while minimizing unnecessary interruptions.

Notifications are event-driven and originate from other business domains.

---

# Objectives

The Notifications domain exists to:

- Keep users informed.
- Encourage timely action.
- Improve marketplace responsiveness.
- Increase inspection conversion.
- Improve agent response times.
- Reduce uncertainty.

---

# Design Principles

## Event Driven

Notifications are never created manually.

Every notification originates from a business event.

Examples:

- Listing approved
- Inspection requested
- Verification approved
- Subscription renewed

---

## Actionable

Every notification should answer:

- What happened?
- Why does it matter?
- What should the user do next?

---

## Timely

Notifications should be delivered as soon as reasonably possible.

---

## Non-Blocking

Failures to deliver notifications must never prevent the underlying business operation from succeeding.

# Notification Lifecycle

```
Generated

↓

Queued

↓

Delivered

↓

Read

↓

Archived
```

Delivery failures should be retried according to retry policies.

---

# Notification Preferences

Users should be able to control:

- Email notifications
- In-app notifications

System-critical notifications may ignore user preferences where legally appropriate.

---

# Notification Queue

Notifications should be processed asynchronously.

Business operations must not wait for notification delivery.

---

# Templates

Every notification should use standardized templates.

Templates include:

- Title
- Body
- CTA (optional)
- Metadata

---

# Deduplication

The platform should avoid sending duplicate notifications for the same event.

---

# Retry Policy

Failed deliveries should be retried.

Permanent failures should be logged for operational review.

---

# Business Rules

| Rule ID | Rule | Severity |
|----------|------|----------|
| BR-NOTIF-001 | Notifications originate from business events. | Critical |
| BR-NOTIF-002 | Notification failures never block business workflows. | Critical |
| BR-NOTIF-003 | System-critical notifications may override preferences. | High |
| BR-NOTIF-004 | Duplicate notifications should be prevented. | High |
| BR-NOTIF-005 | Delivery attempts should be auditable. | High |

# Domain Invariants

- Every notification originates from exactly one business event.
- Notifications are immutable after creation.
- Delivery failures never affect business data.
- Read status is tracked independently.
- Notification history remains available for auditing.

---

# Edge Cases

Examples include:

- User disables email notifications.
- Notification generated for suspended user.
- Duplicate event received.
- Notification generated after account deletion.
- Delayed email delivery.

---

# Failure Modes

Examples include:

- Email provider unavailable.
- Queue backlog.
- Template rendering failure.
- Duplicate delivery.
- Notification worker crash.

The system should retry where appropriate and log failures for review.
