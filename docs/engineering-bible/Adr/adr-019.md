# ADR-019 — Adopt Property Inspection as an Independent Business Workflow

## Status

Accepted

---

# Context

Viewing a property is one of the final and most important stages in the real estate journey.

Many marketplaces simply expose a phone number and expect buyers and agents to coordinate externally.

This approach provides little visibility into:

- Scheduled inspections
- Missed appointments
- Disputes
- User engagement
- Agent responsiveness

Inspection scheduling is a business capability rather than merely a messaging feature.

---

# Decision

Ruvo will model property inspections as an independent business domain.

Inspection requests are separate from:

- Listings
- Messaging
- Verification
- Payments

Every inspection has its own lifecycle, business rules, audit history, and notifications.

---

# Inspection Lifecycle

```
Requested

↓

Pending Confirmation

↓

Confirmed

↓

Completed

OR

Cancelled

OR

Expired
```

Every transition generates notifications and audit events.

---

# Responsibilities

The Inspection domain owns:

- Appointment requests
- Scheduling
- Confirmation
- Rescheduling
- Cancellation
- Completion
- Inspection history

Listings do not manage appointments directly.

---

# Business Rules

- One inspection belongs to one listing.
- One inspection has one requester.
- Agents may approve or decline requests.
- Completed inspections cannot be edited.
- Cancelled inspections remain in history.

---

# Rationale

## Separation of Concerns

Scheduling is operational.

Listings describe properties.

Messaging facilitates communication.

Each domain remains independent.

---

## Better User Experience

Users receive structured appointment management rather than informal conversations.

---

## Operational Visibility

The platform can measure:

- Confirmation rates
- Completion rates
- Agent responsiveness
- Missed appointments

---

## Future Extensibility

The workflow supports future capabilities including:

- Calendar synchronization
- Reminder notifications
- Route planning
- Digital check-in
- Inspection feedback

without redesigning the architecture.

---

# Alternatives Considered

## Messaging Only

### Pros

- Simple.

### Cons

- No scheduling workflow.
- No analytics.
- No reminders.
- Difficult tracking.

Decision:

Rejected.

---

## External Scheduling Services

### Pros

- Reduced implementation effort.

### Cons

- Fragmented experience.
- Limited control.
- Poor integration.

Decision:

Rejected.

---

# Consequences

## Positive

- Better scheduling experience.
- Improved operational insight.
- Richer analytics.
- Future extensibility.

## Negative

- Additional engineering effort.
- More operational workflows.

---

# Non-Negotiable Constraints

- Inspection history is immutable.
- State transitions are auditable.
- Notifications accompany major events.
- Inspection records remain independent of messaging.

---

# Decision Impact Assessment

## Product Impact

Creates a professional appointment management experience that increases user confidence.

## Engineering Impact

Introduces an independent scheduling domain with clear boundaries.

## Operational Impact

Provides measurable operational metrics and structured workflows.

# AI Implementation Guidance

Treat inspections as first-class business entities.

Never embed inspection state inside the Listings domain.

Maintain explicit lifecycle transitions.

## Definition of Done

Inspection requests progress through a managed lifecycle, remain independently auditable, and support future scheduling enhancements.
