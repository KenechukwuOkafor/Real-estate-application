# ADR-004 — Adopt Paystack as the Primary Payment Provider

## Status

**Accepted**

---

# Context

Ruvo requires a secure, reliable, and locally optimized payment provider to support its subscription-based business model.

The platform needs to collect recurring subscription payments from Nigerian real estate agents while maintaining accurate financial records and minimizing operational overhead.

The payment platform must support:

- Card payments
- Bank transfers
- Webhook notifications
- Transaction verification
- Refund workflows
- Subscription billing
- Secure payment processing
- Compliance with Nigerian payment infrastructure

Payments must never expose card information to Ruvo's servers.

---

# Decision

Ruvo will use **Paystack** as its primary payment provider.

Paystack will be responsible for:

- Payment collection
- Payment authorization
- Payment verification
- Transaction processing
- Secure payment pages
- Webhook events
- Refund APIs
- Customer payment methods

Ruvo remains responsible for:

- Subscription state
- Entitlements
- Invoice references
- Payment history
- Access control
- Business rules

---

# Architectural Responsibilities

## Paystack owns

- Payment processing
- Card security
- PCI compliance
- Bank integrations
- Transaction settlement
- Payment authorization

---

## Ruvo owns

- Subscription plans
- Agent entitlements
- Billing history
- Feature access
- Renewal rules
- Expiry handling
- Financial reporting

Payment infrastructure is separated from business logic.

---

# Rationale

## 1. Nigerian Market Alignment

Ruvo launches in Nigeria.

Paystack is deeply integrated into the Nigerian financial ecosystem and provides a familiar checkout experience for users.

---

## 2. Security

Sensitive payment information never passes through Ruvo.

Card details are handled entirely by Paystack.

This significantly reduces security and compliance requirements.

---

## 3. Mature Developer Experience

Paystack provides:

- REST APIs
- Webhooks
- SDKs
- Excellent documentation
- Stable infrastructure

These reduce implementation complexity.

---

## 4. Subscription Support

The business model relies on recurring subscriptions for verified agents.

Paystack provides the primitives needed to support recurring billing while allowing Ruvo to maintain its own subscription logic.

---

## 5. Operational Simplicity

The engineering team does not need to build or maintain payment infrastructure.

Financial operations remain focused on business workflows rather than payment processing.

---

# Alternatives Considered

## Flutterwave

### Pros

- Strong Nigerian presence.
- Broad payment options.
- Mature APIs.

### Cons

- Similar capabilities to Paystack.
- Team familiarity favored Paystack.
- Existing architectural decisions already align with Paystack.

**Decision:** Rejected.

---

## Stripe

### Pros

- Excellent APIs.
- Global reach.
- Large ecosystem.

### Cons

- Less aligned with Ruvo's initial Nigerian launch.
- Introduces complexity that is unnecessary for the MVP.

**Decision:** Deferred for future international expansion.

---

## Custom Payment Processing

### Pros

None meaningful.

### Cons

- Impossible to justify.
- Massive security burden.
- Regulatory complexity.
- PCI compliance requirements.

**Decision:** Strongly Rejected.

---

# Consequences

## Positive

- Secure payment processing.
- Familiar user experience.
- Reduced engineering effort.
- Faster MVP delivery.
- Easier financial reconciliation.

---

## Negative

- Dependency on Paystack availability.
- Webhook reliability becomes critical.
- Vendor-specific APIs require maintenance.

---

# Non-Negotiable Constraints

- Ruvo never stores card information.
- Subscription activation only occurs after verified payment.
- Webhooks must be cryptographically verified.
- Payment success pages are not trusted as proof of payment.
- Subscription state changes only after backend verification.

---

# Failure Scenarios

## Payment succeeds but webhook is delayed

Subscription remains pending until the webhook is verified.

---

## User closes browser after payment

Backend reconciliation updates the subscription once Paystack confirms payment.

---

## Duplicate webhook delivery

Webhook handlers must be idempotent.

Duplicate events must not create duplicate subscriptions.

---

## Payment verification fails

Subscription remains inactive.

Engineering logs the failure.

Operations may investigate if required.

---

# Decision Impact Assessment

## Product Impact

Provides trusted payment methods familiar to Nigerian users while enabling a subscription-based business model.

---

## Engineering Impact

Reduces implementation complexity by delegating payment processing while preserving full control over subscription logic.

---

## Operational Impact

Simplifies reconciliation and customer support through standardized payment events and transaction references.

---

## Risk Assessment

### Risks

- Webhook delivery failures.
- Third-party outages.
- Transaction disputes.

### Mitigations

- Idempotent webhook processing.
- Retry mechanisms.
- Periodic payment reconciliation.
- Comprehensive audit logging.

---

# Future Considerations

Potential future enhancements include:

- Flutterwave as a secondary provider.
- Stripe for international expansion.
- Multi-currency support.
- Automated invoicing.
- Tax calculation.

These enhancements should preserve the abstraction between payment processing and subscription management.

# AI Implementation Guidance

## Context

This ADR establishes Paystack as the approved payment provider for Ruvo.

AI coding agents must assume that all payment processing occurs through Paystack while all subscription logic remains within Ruvo.

## Non-Negotiable Rules

- Never process card data directly.
- Always verify payments server-side.
- Treat webhooks as the authoritative source of payment events.
- Ensure webhook handlers are idempotent.
- Keep payment infrastructure separate from subscription business rules.

## Common Mistakes

- Activating subscriptions from frontend callbacks.
- Trusting redirect URLs without backend verification.
- Failing to verify webhook signatures.
- Mixing payment logic with entitlement logic.

## Definition of Done

Payments are processed through Paystack, verified on the backend, recorded in audit logs, and result in consistent subscription state transitions.
